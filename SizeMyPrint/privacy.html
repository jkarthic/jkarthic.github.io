Privacy Policy

Size My Print · Effective October 21, 2026

Size My Print does not collect your personal information. Photos you add are processed on your iPhone or iPad. They are not uploaded to the developer, and the app has no account, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Who we are

Size My Print is an iOS app that places photos on a paper size so you can print them at a real-world size, or save a PDF or JPEG. It is published by Karthick Jeyapal (“we”, “us”).

This policy describes the information the app accesses, how that information is used, and how you can remove it. It is written for the App Store privacy requirements in Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines, including guideline 5.1.

Information the app accesses

The app accesses only what you choose to bring into a page, plus one preference stored on your device.

  • Photos you select. “Add Photos” opens Apple’s photo picker. You choose the images to add. The app does not browse or upload your photo library, and it does not ask for access to your entire library.
  • Files you import. “Files” opens the system file picker. You choose image files. The app reads only the files you select.
  • A measurement preference. The app remembers whether you measure in inches or millimeters. That choice is stored on your device with the system settings store (UserDefaults). It is not tied to your name, Apple ID, or any other identifier, and it is not sent to us.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, camera, microphone, health data, or payment information. There is no sign-in.

How photos are used

Selected photos are copied into the app’s temporary storage so you can place, crop, rotate, flip, and size them while the page is open. A smaller preview is kept in memory for editing. The full image is read from that temporary file only when you print or export.

Those copies stay on your device. We do not receive them, store them on a server, or use them to train models, build a profile, or show ads.

Temporary copies can be removed by deleting the photos from the page, leaving the page, or deleting the app. iOS may also clear the app’s temporary storage on its own. A copy you save through the share sheet, or a page you print, remains wherever you sent it.

Printing

Print uses Apple’s AirPrint sheet. The page is sent to the printer you select on your network. We do not receive the print job, and we do not see which printer you use. Printing is handled by iOS and your printer. Their handling of the job is covered by Apple’s and the printer maker’s own terms.

Saving and sharing

Save PDF and Save JPEG prepare a file in temporary storage and open the system share sheet. The file leaves the app only if you choose a destination, such as Files, Mail, Messages, or another app you pick. We do not choose that destination and we do not receive a copy.

Whatever app or service you share to processes that file under its own privacy policy.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, sell, rent, or share personal information. In particular, the app does not include:

  • Accounts or identifiers linked to you
  • Analytics, crash reporting, or diagnostics sent off the device
  • Advertising or measurement SDKs
  • Tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies
  • The App Tracking Transparency prompt, because the app does not track
  • Third-party SDKs that receive your photos or usage
  • A server of ours that your photos or preferences are sent to

Under Apple’s definition, data is “collected” when it is sent off the device in a way that lets the developer or a partner access it for longer than it takes to service the request. Size My Print does not do that. In App Store Connect, the app’s privacy disclosure is Data Not Collected.

Third parties

The app uses Apple system features only: the photo picker, the Files picker, AirPrint, and the share sheet. We do not give those features, or any other party, permission to use your photos for their own purposes. If you print or share, the printer or the destination you choose receives the page because you sent it there, not because we shared it.

Retention and deletion

We have no account and no server copy of your photos, pages, or preferences, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf.

  • Photos on a page. Remove them in the app, or delete the app. Temporary copies are not kept as a library inside Size My Print.
  • Measurement preference. Delete the app to remove it. You can also change the unit in the app at any time.
  • Files you exported or pages you printed. Those copies are outside the app. Delete them from the place you saved them, or dispose of the print, if you no longer want them.

You can stop the app from accessing further photos at any time by not selecting more images. The photo picker asks you each time; the app does not keep ongoing access to your library.

Children

Size My Print is a general utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is sent to us, we do not create a profile of a child.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you the right to access, correct, or delete personal information a developer holds, or to object to certain uses. We do not hold your personal information, so there is no profile to access, correct, export, or erase from our side. Deleting the app removes the on-device preference and temporary files described above.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act. We do not process personal information for targeted advertising.

Security

Photos and the measurement preference stay in the app sandbox that iOS provides. There is no password and no account because the app does not transmit your content to us. No method of storage is perfectly secure; protect your device with a passcode, and be deliberate about where you print or share a page.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and change the effective date above. The current version is the one published at this URL. Material changes will also be reflected in the app’s App Store privacy details.
